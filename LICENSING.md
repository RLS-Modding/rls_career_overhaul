RLS Modding licenses the original source files listed in
[RLS-AGPL-SCOPE.md](licenses/RLS-AGPL-SCOPE.md) under **GNU AGPL version 3 only**
(`AGPL-3.0-only`), with the [BeamNG additional permission](licenses/RLS-BeamNG-Permission.txt).
The complete AGPL text is in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

The additional permission allows those files to work with BeamNG and its
bCDDL components without requiring the game to be licensed under AGPL. It
retains the AGPL's source and notice obligations for the RLS material and
does not grant rights in third-party code or assets.

## BeamNG-derived source

BeamNG source covered by **bCDDL 1.1**, including modifications and new files
containing covered code, retains that license. Moving code into an RLS
namespace does not change its terms. Preserve upstream notices, identify
contributors to modifications, and meet the source-distribution requirements.
The full license is in [licenses/bCDDL-1.1.txt](licenses/bCDDL-1.1.txt).

Do not replace bCDDL notices with AGPL headers. An RLS contributor notice in
a bCDDL file identifies RLS modifications; it does not change the file's license.

## Included components and assets

Existing third-party grants and notices remain in effect. See
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md), the
[tire integration notice](licenses/tyre-thermals-NOTICE.md), and the bundled
component licenses. RLS's version choice and additional permission do not
replace another author's grant or waive their conditions.

The exact-path RLS grant does not assign a new license to unlisted files,
images, textures, models, audio or fonts. These retain their applicable terms;
missing notices do not mean public domain. This scope does not narrow any
existing license coverage or certify rights in every bundled item.

## Contributions and redistribution

Preserve applicable licenses, attribution, and modification notices. Modified
AGPL work must carry the relevant change notices and dates. Provide
Corresponding Source as required by the AGPL, including required build and
installation inputs where applicable. Follow section 13 when a modified
version supports remote network interaction to which that section applies.

New original RLS files require an explicit scope entry and source notice;
filenames and folder names alone do not establish their license. The RLS
grant is version 3 only, without an automatic election of later versions.
The unchanged license text's application example does not alter that choice.
